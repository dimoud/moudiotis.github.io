Dossier Handling

Many vehicle cases get stuck on a single document: a certificate the customs office asks for, a technical memorandum the Transport Directorate needs, a reply to a question from the Technical Service. We take on that part, meaning the technical text, the engineer signature and the contact with the authority, so the file can move forward.

Who it is for

  • Importers, dealers and vehicle manufacturers.
  • Vehicle owners whose file is delayed or has been rejected.
  • Conversion companies and body builders.
  • Customs brokers and lawyers who need technical documentation.

Certification dossier management

Before inspection bodies, technical services and testing laboratories, the office represents you and provides technical support. We collect what is needed from the manufacturer, map standards and regulations, answer the authority's remarks and follow the file until it closes. With manufacturers and technical services abroad we work in English.

Certificates and technical memoranda

We issue engineer certificates, solemn declarations under Article 8 of Law 1599/1986 and technical memoranda for customs, KTEO, Transport Directorates and registration dossiers. Every certificate rests on what we checked: inspection, manufacturer documents, measurements. We do not sign what cannot be documented, and that is what makes the certificate accepted.

When the file was rejected

We read the rejection, identify the provision it relies on and check whether it applies the way it was applied. If there is room, we reply to the authority with documentation. If the rejection stands, we tell you plainly and show you what can be done instead.

THE RIGHT QUESTION TO THE RIGHT AUTHORITY

Many delays start with a request that went to the wrong authority or relied on a provision that has since changed. Before every filing we check which provision is in force today and which authority is competent.

WHAT OUR OFFICE HANDLES
  • 01We listen to the case and read what you have: registrations, CoC, approvals, correspondence with the authority.
  • 02We check the legislation in force and which authority is competent.
  • 03We draft the certificate, declaration or technical memorandum, with an on-site inspection where needed.
  • 04We contact the authority or body and follow the file until it closes.

Send us what the authority asked for and the documents you have. We will tell you what is needed and whether it can be documented.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
I only need one engineer certificate. Do you take that on?

Yes, as long as its content can be documented by inspection or by documents. We tell you in advance what we will need.

My file was rejected. Is there a way forward?

Often there is: a missing document, a provision read differently. Sometimes not, and then we tell you before you pay for something that will not change the outcome.

Do you work with foreign manufacturers?

Yes. We deal with manufacturers and technical services abroad for the documents a Greek file needs.

Do you work outside Athens?

Yes. We handle cases across Greece, and for clients abroad.
