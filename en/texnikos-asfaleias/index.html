Every business in Greece that employs even one person must have a Safety Engineer. Law 3850/2010 was codified in 2025 into the Labour Law Code (PD 62/2025) and amended by Law 5239/2025. The obligation remains; what changed is how businesses are classified and some of the rules on assignment.

Who it is for

  • Every business with employees, from a small shop to industry.
  • Employers who want to check whether their current coverage is still right after the changes.
  • Businesses with construction sites, warehouses, workshops or machinery.

Category A, B or C

A business is classified into a risk category based on its activity code (KAD), under the new table introduced by Law 5239/2025. The category and the number of employees give the hours the Safety Engineer must work each year, subject to a minimum. Because the table changed, the classification is worth rechecking even if the business is already covered.

Can the employer do it personally?

In category B or C businesses with up to 20 employees, the employer may, from 1.1.2026, carry out Safety Engineer duties personally, provided the legal requirements are met (Article 502, PD 62/2025). In every other case a Safety Engineer with the qualifications required for the business category is appointed.

What the Safety Engineer does

  • Written occupational risk assessment (GEEK) for every workstation.
  • Instructions to the employer, recorded in the instructions book; the law now provides for keeping it electronically on OPS-SEPE.
  • Training and information of employees.
  • Evacuation plan and emergency instructions.
  • Support after a workplace accident, which is reported within 24 hours to the Labour Inspectorate and e-EFKA.
  • Preparation for a Labour Inspectorate inspection.

The assignment is declared online on OPS-SEPE, and the Safety Engineer's hours are entered on the staff schedule.

THE MOST COMMON INSPECTION FINDING

A Safety Engineer on paper only: no risk assessment, no instructions, no hours on the staff schedule. At a Labour Inspectorate inspection this does not cover the business. The contract is worth something only if real work on site comes with it.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
I have one employee. Do I need a Safety Engineer?

Yes. The obligation applies to every business with employees, whatever their number. In small category B or C businesses the employer may, under conditions, take it on personally.

How many hours are needed?

It depends on the business category and the number of employees, with a minimum number of hours per year. Tell us your activity code and headcount and we will give you the calculation.

Do I also need an Occupational Physician?

An Occupational Physician is required, as a rule, from 50 employees upwards. We confirm it for your specific case.

Which areas do you cover?

Athens and Thessaloniki. For other areas, ask us.
