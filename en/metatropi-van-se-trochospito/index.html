VAN → MOTOR CARAVAN

Converting a van into a motor caravan is possible. It is possible under conditions, though, and it ends with an inspection and a new registration certificate. Before any work starts, we check whether your vehicle and the conversion you plan can be approved.

Call us210 756 1836 Request a quote Send a photo

Who it is for

  • Van owners who want to turn it into a camper.
  • Owners who have already converted and want to legalise it.
  • Workshops building motor caravans.
ASSESSMENT FIRST, BUILD SECOND

A conversion built without first checking the conditions may not be approvable as it stands. Talk to us before you start.

WHAT OUR OFFICE HANDLES
  • 01We review the vehicle and the conversion you want.
  • 02We tell you whether it is possible and under which conditions.
  • 03We prepare the study and the dossier.
  • 04We file, follow the inspection and hand you the new registration.

Send us the van registration certificate and a description of the conversion. We will tell you whether it can be done.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
I already converted it. Can it be legalised?

It depends on what was done and how. We examine the vehicle and tell you plainly whether, and what, is needed.

Can any van be converted?

Not in every case. That is why the assessment comes before the build.

Do you work outside Athens?

Yes, across Greece.
