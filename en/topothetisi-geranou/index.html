VEHICLE + CRANE

A crane changes the vehicle: it adds weight, shifts load between the axles and changes the vehicle stability while it works. That is why fitting it needs a study and ends with an inspection and a new registration certificate.

Call us210 756 1836 Request a quote Send a photo

Who it is for

  • Vehicle owners fitting a crane.
  • Companies selling and fitting cranes.
  • Buyers of a used crane vehicle that was never declared properly.

What the study checks

New axle load distribution, payload, mounting of the crane to the chassis according to the vehicle manufacturer's guidelines, and stability in operation. The crane, as a machine, carries CE marking from its manufacturer; the study concerns its installation on this particular vehicle.

STUDY FIRST, INSTALLATION SECOND

If the crane is fitted before the loads are checked, it may turn out the vehicle is overloaded or the chassis needs reinforcing.

WHAT OUR OFFICE HANDLES
  • 01We review the vehicle and the crane you chose.
  • 02We calculate loads, payload and stability.
  • 03We prepare the study with mounting drawings.
  • 04We file, follow the inspection and hand you the new registration.

Send us the vehicle registration certificate and the crane details. We will tell you whether they match and what is needed.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
The crane has CE marking. Is that not enough?

CE covers the crane. It does not show whether this vehicle carries it safely. The study shows that.

I bought a used crane vehicle. What should I check?

That the crane is recorded on the registration certificate. If not, we look at what is needed to legalise it.
