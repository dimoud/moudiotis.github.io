CHASSIS + SUPERSTRUCTURE

Crane trucks, catering units, ambulances, mobile clinics, snow ploughs, platforms: vehicles built to perform a job rather than to carry a load. Registering them does not follow one procedure but five, and choosing the right one is settled before the file is even opened.

Who it is for

  • Private owners with a caravan, a wheelchair-accessible vehicle, or their own vehicle converted for a special use.
  • Sole traders and small businesses — catering unit, mobile shop, crane truck, roadside assistance vehicle.
  • Bodywork manufacturers and fleet operators.
  • Municipalities and public bodies.

Which vehicles count as special purpose

The official definition covers vehicles in categories M, N or O “having specific technical features in order to perform a function which requires special arrangements or equipment”. In practice:

  • Crane trucks, aerial platforms, lifting platforms and roadside assistance vehicles.
  • Mobile shops and catering units — on a truck or on a trailer.
  • Ambulances, hearses, wheelchair-accessible vehicles.
  • Mobile units: medical, blood donation, library, classroom, exhibition, cinema.
  • Snow ploughs, salt spreaders, sweepers, sprayers, pump carriers, pile drivers.
  • Armoured and cash-in-transit vehicles, vehicles carrying exceptional loads.

The official list runs to 59 sub-categories. If the vehicle matches none of them, it is not a special purpose vehicle — and the file belongs somewhere else.

Where the time goes

“Special purpose” sounds like a single procedure. It is not. There are five distinct routes, each with its own legislation, its own competent authority and its own forms.

The same vehicle may go to the Transport Directorate, to the regional Technical Works Directorate, or to an accredited Technical Service — depending on whether it carries goods, whether it is a trailer, whether it already holds a Greek registration, or whether this is a first registration.

The wrong route means the wrong authority, the wrong legislation and a file started over. That choice is not made by eye; it turns on the category, the intended use and the history of the particular vehicle.

And something many people learn late

The workshop carrying out the conversion has to meet specific requirements — licensed premises, a quality system, welding and welder certification. A conversion done in an uncertified workshop will not pass, however good the work.

THE COSTLIEST MISTAKE HAPPENS BEFORE THE START

The route is chosen on day one and it determines everything: authority, legislation, forms, timing. A file that started down the wrong route is not corrected — it is rebuilt. That is why the first conversation is about the vehicle and its use, not about paperwork.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Can my trailer become a catering unit?

Yes. A mobile shop is an expressly provided sub-category. For trailers the route is fixed, whatever work they perform. Beyond the conversion you will also need health authority licensing, which is a separate matter.

Can I turn my box truck into a motorhome?

No. A category N vehicle cannot be converted into category M — two independent ministry circulars, eight years apart, reach the same conclusion. Only fitting a motorhome body onto an open flatbed up to 3,500 kg is allowed. An M1 van, on the other hand, can be converted.

The vehicle is already registered. Does it need type approval again?

For converting a vehicle that already holds a Greek registration, no — that was settled at first registration. The study covers the conversion. Type approval is required for a first registration.

I have registered an ambulance. Can I put it on the road?

Registration is one part of it. Operating as an ambulance also requires certification by the national emergency service and certified crew. It is a separate regime with its own documentation, varying by the legal form of the operator.

How long does it take?

Our own turnaround is short: the dossier is prepared and filed within a few days. After that the clock belongs to the authority and is measured in weeks. We do not promise dates that are not ours to keep — but we tell you at every step where the file stands.
