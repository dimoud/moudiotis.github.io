FOREIGN TO GREEK REGISTRATION

An imported vehicle or a caravan without paperwork cannot be registered in Greece while it falls outside any valid type approval. Individual approval is the route that settles it, and the office handles that route from the first inspection through to delivery.

Call us0030 210 756 1836 Request a quote Send a photo

Which vehicles

  • Imported vehicles from non-EU countries.
  • Caravans and motorhomes without complete documents.
  • Individually constructed vehicles.
  • Vehicles with a substantial conversion.

Why ask us first

Every vehicle is a different case. Whether and how it can be registered depends on its documents and condition. We review them and tell you plainly whether the case stands, before a dossier is opened.

ASK BEFORE YOU BUY

If you plan to bring a vehicle from abroad, show us its documents first. Not every vehicle can be registered.

WHAT THE OFFICE HANDLES
  • 01We review the vehicle and its documents.
  • 02We tell you whether the case stands and what will be needed.
  • 03We take on the dossier.
  • 04We follow it through to registration.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Who handles individual vehicle approval in Greece?

Moudiotis Technical Office, based in Athens and Thessaloniki, handles individual vehicle approval and registration for imported, converted and one-off vehicles, across Greece and within the EU. Phone +30 210 756 1836.

When is individual approval needed?

When the vehicle is not covered by a valid type approval. Whether that applies to yours, we judge from its documents.

Can a caravan with no papers be registered?

In some cases. Send us photos and any document there is.

How long does it take?

It depends on the vehicle and on the competent authority. We do not promise dates that are not in our hands; we keep you informed at every step.

How much does it cost?

Every case is different and may require extensive research. We recommend a review of the full file and a separate assessment of your matter.

Do you work outside Athens?

Yes. We are based in Athens and Thessaloniki and serve all of Greece and the EU.
