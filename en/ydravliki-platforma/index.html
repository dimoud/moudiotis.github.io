A hydraulic platform, the familiar tail lift at the back of a truck or the lifting table at a loading dock, is a machine that lifts loads with people working right next to it. Fitted to a vehicle, it changes the vehicle weight, the load on each axle and the details on the registration certificate. That is why it needs a study, not just a good workshop.

Who it is for

  • Owners of delivery and refrigerated trucks fitting a tail lift.
  • Body builders and workshops installing platforms.
  • Businesses with loading docks and lifting tables in warehouses and shops.

On a vehicle: what changes on the registration

A tail lift is not an accessory. At the periodic roadworthiness test (KTEO) the inspector checks that the tail lift is recorded on the registration certificate, or that a relevant note from the competent authority exists, and that the mechanism works properly. A tail lift fitted without being declared will show up at the KTEO.

The platform usually adds several hundred kilograms at the rear of the vehicle. Load shifts from the front to the rear axle and the payload drops. The study checks that no axle exceeds its permitted load and that the mounting to the chassis follows the vehicle manufacturer's body builder guidelines.

The machine itself: CE and standards

The platform is a machine under Directive 2006/42/EC, transposed into Greek law by PD 57/2010. Its manufacturer supplies it with CE marking and an EC declaration of conformity. For platforms mounted on vehicles the harmonised standard is EN 1756-1; for fixed lifting tables it is EN 1570-1. From 20 January 2027 the Directive is replaced by the Machinery Regulation (EU) 2023/1230.

CE covers the machine. It does not tell you whether this particular vehicle can carry its weight or whether the chassis connection is right. The installation study shows that.

Is it required?

On a vehicle in use, the platform must appear on the registration certificate for the vehicle to pass the KTEO, and the certificate is updated by the Transport Directorate. At a fixed installation, the employer must provide equipment that is safe for workers and keep it maintained; documentation of the installation is among the first things asked for at an inspection.

STUDY FIRST, INSTALLATION SECOND

If the platform is fitted before the loads are checked, it may turn out that the rear axle is overloaded or the chassis needs reinforcing. The fix then happens on a finished build and costs more. Send us the registration certificate and the platform details before work starts.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
I already fitted a tail lift without declaring it. What now?

The tail lift has to be added to the registration certificate. We examine the installation as it is, run the calculations and put the dossier together. If anything needs correcting, we tell you before filing.

Will my payload go down?

Yes. The platform weight is added to the unladen mass and comes off the load you can carry. The calculation shows exactly how much.

The platform has CE marking. Is that not enough?

CE covers the machine itself. It does not show whether this vehicle can carry its weight or whether the chassis mounting is right. The study documents that.

Do you work outside Athens?

Yes. We handle cases across Greece, and for clients abroad.
