TYPE APPROVAL DOSSIER

What it solves

Through the Notification Certificate the competent Greek authority accepts a type approval, so that vehicles covered by it can be registered in the country. At the same time the technical characteristics of the type are recorded in the Technical Vehicle Archive, the register from which the Transport Directorate draws the figures printed on the registration certificate.

That entry is not an administrative detail. Whatever goes into the register will appear on the certificate of every vehicle of that type, and correcting it later calls for a fresh procedure.

Who this concerns

  • Importers and dealerships bringing vehicles in for registration.
  • Bodybuilders and superstructure manufacturers — refrigerated bodies, tippers, cranes, box bodies, special trailers.
  • Vehicle and caravan traders handling a series of the same type.
  • Fleet operators, along with public bodies registering in volume.

Where files usually stall

The electronic technical characteristics file sits at the centre of the dossier and is, in our experience, the most frequent reason for a return. It calls for a specific structure per category and consistency on every line.

Next comes the matching of type, variant and version. Where an approval covers several variants, the link to the CoC of the particular vehicle has to be exact. The same goes for the approval number: extensions and corrections form part of it, and leaving them out at even one point in the file creates a problem.

Finally there are compliance requirements that shift over time, and discrepancies between the approval, the CoC and the technical file — even where these originate with the manufacturer.

Timing

For passenger cars and light commercial vehicles, that is categories M1 and N1, a fast route applies which indicatively completes within a few days. For the remaining categories the authority’s deadline runs to 50 days from the submission of a complete file. In both cases completeness remains the deciding factor.

THE ERROR DOES NOT STAY WITH ONE VEHICLE

On a single vehicle an incorrect figure is corrected once. On a type notification the same figure carries over to every vehicle registered under that type. For that reason the dossier is cross-checked before it is filed.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
I already have a CoC. Is that not enough?

Not on its own. The CoC certifies that the particular vehicle conforms to an approved type, but the type has to have been notified and recorded in Greece before it can be registered.

What is the TAO and why does it concern me?

It is the Technical Vehicle Archive of the Ministry of Infrastructure and Transport, the register of approved types. The Transport Directorate draws the details of the registration certificate from it.

How long does it take?

For M1 and N1 a fast route applies, indicatively a few days. For the remaining categories the authority’s deadline is 50 days from the submission of a complete file. These are the authority’s deadlines rather than a delivery commitment.

Is my type already in the TAO?

We check before taking the work on. If the type, or a related one, is already recorded, the procedure can be considerably shorter. Send us the vehicle details.

Do you also handle the registration afterwards?

Yes. The office covers the chain from type approval and the Notification Certificate through to final registration and the registration certificate, including special purpose vehicles and conversions.

Do you work outside Attica?

Yes — Athens and Thessaloniki, taking on cases throughout Greece.
