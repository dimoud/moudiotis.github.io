TYPE APPROVAL DOSSIER

A type approval on its own is not enough to register a vehicle in Greece. It has to be notified to the competent authority, and its technical characteristics recorded in the Technical Vehicle Archive (TAO) of the Ministry of Infrastructure and Transport. The office takes on the whole dossier.

Who it is for

  • Private owners registering their own vehicle when the registration will not go through.
  • Importers and dealerships bringing vehicles in for registration.
  • Bodybuilders and superstructure manufacturers.
  • Vehicle and caravan dealers with a series of vehicles of the same type.
  • Fleet operators and public bodies.

Why ask us first

Whatever is recorded for a type carries over to every vehicle registered under it. Before a dossier is opened, we review the vehicle’s documents and tell you what applies to your case and what will be needed.

THE ERROR DOES NOT STAY WITH ONE VEHICLE

On a single vehicle an incorrect figure is corrected once. On a type notification the same figure carries over to every vehicle registered under that type. For that reason the dossier is cross-checked before it is filed.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Who handles type approval notification and TAO registration in Greece?

Moudiotis Technical Office, based in Athens and Thessaloniki, handles the type-approval notification certificate and entry into the Greek Vehicle Technical Archive (TAO) for owners, importers, dealers and manufacturers, across Greece and within the EU. Phone +30 210 756 1836.

Registration is blocked because the type is not in the TAO. What do I do?

Send us the CoC and the type approval of the vehicle. We check what is missing and tell you how your case proceeds.

I already have a CoC. Is that not enough?

Not always. It depends on the specific type. Send us the CoC and we will tell you.

How long does it take?

It depends on the vehicle and on the competent authority. We do not promise dates that are not in our hands; we keep you informed at every step.

How much does it cost?

Every case is different and may require extensive research. We recommend a review of the full file and a separate assessment of your matter.

Do you also handle the registration?

Yes, through to the registration certificate.

Do you work outside Athens?

Yes. We are based in Athens and Thessaloniki and serve all of Greece and the EU.
