O1 / O2 TRAILER

Every trailer needs its own registration certificate and plates. The case can only start if an old Special Towing Note exists. If you hold one, we handle the rest — inspection, chassis number, study, dossier, delivery. Final price €400–430, government fees included.

WITHOUT AN OLD TOWING NOTE, NOTHING CAN BE DONE

The Special Towing Note is the document that opens the procedure. Without it the trailer cannot be registered by this route, however good its condition. If you are not sure what you are holding, send us a photograph of the document and we will tell you plainly whether it goes forward.

Which trailers need a licence

All of them. The law draws no line between the small trailer carrying a dinghy and a company’s heavy unit.

  • O1, up to 750 kg — cargo box, boat, motorcycle, camping.
  • O2, up to 3,500 kg — caravan, car transporter, horse trailer.

You do not have to go anywhere

We come to the trailer, inspect it, stamp the chassis number, prepare the study and file the dossier. You collect the licence and plates.

UNTIL THE LICENCE IS ISSUED, THE TRAILER STAYS OFF THE ROAD

Until the licence and plates are issued, use on the road is expressly prohibited. If stopped, the trailer is treated as uninsured and unregistered, with the penalties the Highway Code provides. Note also that a trailer is insured separately from the towing vehicle — check with your insurer.

WHAT THE OFFICE HANDLES
  • 01Inspection and recording. We examine the trailer and record every characteristic accurately, so the licence is issued correctly the first time.
  • 02We stamp the new seventeen-digit chassis number and fit the manufacturer’s plate.
  • 03We prepare the technical description with photographic documentation, the chassis number imprint and all supporting papers.
  • 04We file the complete dossier with the competent authority and follow it through to the licence and plates.

Do you have a trailer, cargo box or caravan without plates? Send us photographs and whatever paperwork exists — we will tell you whether it can be done and what it costs, at no charge.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
What does it cost in total?

The final price is €400 to €430, government fees included. Nothing further comes up along the way.

How long does it take?

Usually 3 to 10 days once the dossier is complete. The timing depends on the competent authority and on the paperwork that already exists.

Is the old towing note mandatory?

Yes, and it helps: a shorter route exists for exactly these cases. If the note predates 1999, an additional roadworthiness test and a fresh engineer’s declaration are required.

I have no paperwork at all for the trailer.

Then this procedure does not apply. Trailers with no prior document — self-builds, imports — follow a different route, at a different cost and a different timeline. Send photographs and we will tell you plainly what stands up.

Can my car tow it?

For O1 up to 750 kg, yes. For O2 it is allowed for specific uses: horses, boats, caravans, historic and racing vehicles, motorcycles. The mass of the towing vehicle also matters.
