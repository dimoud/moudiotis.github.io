Dossier Handling

Two-wheelers, tricycles and quadricycles do not follow car legislation. They have their own type approval framework, Regulation (EU) 168/2013, with its own sub-categories and its own documentation. The office handles the dossier from classifying the vehicle through to registration in the Technical Vehicle Archive.

The problem it solves

A two-wheeler or quadricycle cannot be plated unless a type approval covers it and its technical data is registered in the Technical Vehicle Archive. That is where importers and dealerships usually get stuck: the European approval exists, but the Greek dossier has not been prepared, or it does not survive review.

Classification decides everything else. A quadricycle can be L6e or L7e depending on mass and speed, and the difference changes both the technical requirements and the driving licence your customer will need. The wrong class means a dossier that comes back.

Which regime applies to your vehicle

Two successive regimes coexist in the paperwork today, and the approval number itself tells them apart:

  • Regulation (EU) 168/2013 — the current framework. The approval number takes the form e9*168/2013*11302*03. The information document follows Annex I to implementing Regulation (EU) 901/2014.
  • Directive 2002/24/EC — repealed, but approvals issued under it still accompany vehicles. There the information document is Annex II to the Directive.

Before anything is filled in, it must be established which of the two applies to that specific approval. This is one of the points where a dossier quietly fails: the fields look identical, but they are drawn from a different paragraph of a different document.

Nationally, the type approval procedures for category L are laid down by Joint Ministerial Decision 129846/2021 (Gov. Gazette B΄ 2384/7.6.2021). Registration certificates and plates are governed by the new Greek Road Traffic Code, Law 5209/2025.

Is it necessary?

Yes, where a new vehicle type is being registered in Greece. Without a notified type approval and registered technical data, the Transport Directorate will not issue a registration certificate. For a one-off vehicle covered by no approval at all, the route is different and is assessed separately.

What makes it difficult

The category L technical file has one hundred and forty-one fields, and each is drawn from a specific paragraph of a specific document. Electric vehicles add their own logic: engine capacity and taxable horsepower stay empty, while the engine type is written as the voltage and power of the electric system. These are the points where a dossier comes back for a reason that is not obvious at first sight.

Then there is the legislation. The delegated regulations supplementing 168/2013 — functional safety, vehicle construction, environmental performance — are amended regularly, and a dossier resting on a superseded version rarely passes.

CLASSIFICATION COMES FIRST

The sub-category is not an administrative detail. It determines the technical requirements that must be evidenced, and for the end owner it determines the driving licence required. A quadricycle registered as L6e when it is in fact L7e creates a problem that surfaces later — for your customer.

FREQUENTLY ASKED QUESTIONS
Which vehicles fall under category L?

Mopeds, motorcycles, tricycles and quadricycles — from the L1e-A powered cycle to the L7e heavy quadricycle. Cars and lorries do not, being governed by Regulation (EU) 2018/858, nor do agricultural tractors.

How do I tell whether a vehicle is L6e or L7e?

Mass in running order and maximum design speed decide it. A light quadricycle L6e means up to 425 kg and up to 45 km/h. Above those limits the vehicle is L7e, with a mass limit of 450 kg for passenger transport and 600 kg for goods. The difference changes both technical requirements and the driving licence.

Is European type approval enough to get plates?

A Greek procedure is also required. The approval is notified and the technical data registered in the Technical Vehicle Archive of the Ministry of Infrastructure and Transport. Until that is done, the Transport Directorate will not issue a registration certificate.

My vehicle has an old approval under Directive 2002/24. Is it valid?

Directive 2002/24/EC has been repealed, but approvals issued under it still accompany vehicles and are treated accordingly. Which regime applies is visible from the format of the approval number, and it determines where each technical value is drawn from.

Does the same apply to electric two-wheelers?

Yes, with differences in how the file is completed. On pure electric vehicles, engine capacity and taxable horsepower stay empty, while the engine type is declared as the voltage and power of the electric system, as stated on the approval certificate. Maximum continuous rated power is the figure that decides the sub-category.

What does the state fee cost?

The fee for category L is set by Joint Ministerial Decision 8913/1089/2013 (Gov. Gazette B΄ 501) and is lower than that for cars and trailers. The exact amount depends on the specific procedure and is confirmed before submission. Our own fee is quoted after the initial assessment of the file.

How long does it take?

It is a lengthy procedure. The time it takes depends on the vehicle category, on how complete the file is, and on the authority. We do not give dates that do not depend on us — but we tell you at every step where the file stands.

Do you work outside Athens and with clients abroad?

Yes. The whole process is handled remotely and there is no need to visit the office. We serve clients throughout Greece, as well as manufacturers and importers abroad, in Greek and in English.
